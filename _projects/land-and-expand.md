I'm very interested in AI augmenting the reading and writing process. There are many books I've given up on because they were too dense and convoluted for my feeble brain (looking at you Heidegger) or required a lot of background reading just to make sense of it.

[Land And Expand](https://dmvaldman.github.io/LandAndExpand/) was an experiment in reading Nick Land's [Meltdown essay](https://web.archive.org/web/20250201021520/https://www.ccru.net/swarm1/1_melt.htm) with the help of Gemini 2.5 Pro.

My process was one long-lived [chat](https://gemini.google.com/share/7a1ea86b598c) where I gave Gemini the full essay and we essentially went line by line. Gemini would proactively provide context and anytime I was confused, I would ask questions. At first Gemini gave pretty vanilla responses. It took some iterating and prompt engineering but eventually we got into a flow.

I must say I learned so much more from the AI-enhanced reading process than I ever would have without it. It's like a fast-tracked being a Landian. I think there's a lot of potential for an AI reading experience.
