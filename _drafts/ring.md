Software is dead, so let's build some hardware. A ring enclosure with esp32 board, mic, battery and pushbutton to be exact.

I wanted to go on long walks with a ring device I could speak into that would organize my thoughts and perform agentic tasks. It got pretty good at operating my phone for agentic tasks too.
